inlinestatic

Convective flux of Cartesian momentum through the face between cell c and c + e_n, carried by half the contravariant flux on that face.

Interior faces use the central flux when central (central differencing or LES) and QUICK otherwise; with central differencing the two end faces of an axis are central as well. A face at the low end of an axis or behind a solid cell is QUICK one-sided toward +n, and one at the high end or ahead of a solid cell one-sided toward -n, except across a periodic seam, which keeps the full stencil. A face meeting none of these carries no flux.
