With operation_mode.eulerian_field_source: analytical nothing is solved. The Eulerian field is the case's properties.initial_conditions.source, the definition an initial condition and an inlet take (p33_source), evaluated at each step's time.

1. Activation Path

  • solver.yml -> operation_mode.eulerian_field_source: analytical -> -euler_field_source analytical.
  • case.yml -> properties.initial_conditions.source -> -ic_source_*, as for any initial condition.
  • InitializeEulerianState and the run loop call AnalyticalSolutionEngine.

Each evaluation sets the fluxes on every face, including the boundary faces, Ubcs on every boundary face, and at the cell centres Ucat and P, then finalizes the dummy cells as a solved flow is. As on a time-dependent inlet, a source that does not read t is evaluated once.

2. What Can Be Prescribed

Anything an expression can write, in cartesian or curvilinear components, with these limits:

  • a generator or file term is refused, because the field is re-evaluated, and so is project;
  • a curvilinear source derives Ucat from its fluxes; a cartesian one sets it exactly at the cell centres;
  • a pressure expression [Pa] sets P at every evaluation. Without it P is zero.

The domain is the case's grid, from any grid.mode: no field assigns its own.

Zero flow:

properties:
initial_conditions:
source: {components: cartesian, expression: {u: 0.0, v: 0.0, w: 0.0}}

A uniform stream, with its speed as a constant a study can sweep (case.properties.initial_conditions.source.constants.U):

properties:
initial_conditions:
source: {components: cartesian, constants: {U: 0.1}, expression: {u: U}}

3. Taylor-Green Vortex

properties:
initial_conditions:
source:
components: cartesian
constants: {nu: 0.01}
expression:
u: "sin(x)*cos(y)*cos(z)*exp(-2*nu*t)"
v: "-cos(x)*sin(y)*cos(z)*exp(-2*nu*t)"
pressure: "0.25*(cos(2*x) + cos(2*y))*exp(-4*nu*t)"

with L = U = rho = 1 and nu = 1/Re. examples/interpolation_test runs it on [0, 2 pi]^2 x [0, 0.2 pi], which its grid states.

4. Particle Consistency

When the source is cartesian, reads only x y z t and varies in space, the interpolated particle velocities are compared with it at the particle positions, and interpolation_error.csv is written. A uniform field has no interpolation error to measure, and a source in the grid-line fractions cannot be evaluated at an arbitrary point. FaceFlux interpolation reads the fluxes every analytical field sets.

5. Evidence

Production exercised in examples/interpolation_test, examples/drift_uniform_flow, examples/drift_diffusivity_gradient and examples/brownian_motion. Measured when these fields were selected by name, and reproduced from their sources since: the Brownian cloud of brownian-msd-2026-09-18 in zero flow, the face fluxes on a curved file grid of curvilinear-gcl-2026-09-18 in a uniform stream, and the staged velocity and pressure of solution-monitoring-tgv3d-2026-09-18 for the vortex. On reduced grids over three steps, the source forms match those fields to 2.2e-16 in Ucat, and for the vortex exactly in Ucat, P, particle positions and velocities and the interpolation error. A vortex's FaceFlux interpolation and its fluxes have not been measured against an exact solution.

6. Adding A Field

Write its expressions; no code is needed. A field an expression cannot write belongs in a generator as an initial condition, which is solved from, not prescribed.
