148/**
149 * @brief Forms the deviatoric Germano model tensor `M_ij`.
150 *
151 * `M_ij = -2 Delta^2 ( alpha |S^| S^_ij - (|S| S_ij)^ )`, made trace-free.
152 *
153 * The two terms are structurally different and must stay so. The first is the model
154 * evaluated on the test-filtered strain, the product of separately filtered
155 * quantities. The second is the grid-level model formed pointwise and then
156 * test-filtered, that is, the filter of a product. Their difference is the entire
157 * information content of the dynamic procedure: collapsing them into the same
158 * expression yields a well-scaled, flow-responsive, and wrong coefficient that is
159 * not the Germano-Lilly coefficient and cannot be cited as one.
160 *
161 * @param[in] delta Grid filter width at the cell.
162 * @param[in] alpha Squared test-to-grid filter width ratio.
163 * @param[in] strain_magnitude_filtered Test-filtered strain magnitude `|S|^`.
164 * @param[in] strain_filtered Test-filtered strain tensor `S^_ij`.
165 * @param[in] strain_product_filtered Test filter of the product `(|S| S_ij)^`.
166 * @return The deviatoric model tensor.
167 */
168SymTensor GermanoModelTensor(PetscReal delta, PetscReal alpha,
169 PetscReal strain_magnitude_filtered,
170 SymTensor strain_filtered,
171 SymTensor strain_product_filtered);

248/**
249 * @brief Computes the dynamic Smagorinsky coefficient field for one block.
250 *
251 * Runs the Germano-Lilly procedure: test-filters the resolved field, forms the
252 * Leonard stress and the model tensor, contracts them, averages the two
253 * contractions over the configured directions, limits the quotient, and stores it in
254 * `UserCtx::CS`.
255 *
256 * The stored quantity is the coefficient `C` that multiplies `Delta^2 |S|` in the
257 * eddy viscosity, that is, `Cs^2` rather than `Cs`. It is signed under
258 * ::LES_CLIP_NONE. Reported values are converted to `Cs` at the diagnostic boundary,
259 * where the familiar target near 0.16 applies.
260 *
261 * Only ::DYNAMIC_SMAGORINSKY reaches this routine; the constant model prescribes its
262 * coefficient from configuration and allocates no field. Callers honour
263 * `LESConfig::dynamic_frequency`, and the caller must have refreshed `UserCtx::lUcat`
264 * and its periodic images beforehand.
265 *
266 * @param[in,out] user The user context for a single computational block.
267 * @return PetscErrorCode 0 on success.
268 */
269PetscErrorCode ComputeSmagorinskyConstant(UserCtx *user);
