Detailed Description

Prescribes the Eulerian fields from the case's source instead of solving for them.

With eulerian_field_source: analytical, the field is the initial condition's source, evaluated at the step's time through the operations every target of a source shares: fluxes at the faces (PicurvSourceAddFluxes()) and a bulk velocity (PicurvSourceCellBulkFactor()). A Cartesian source also sets Ucat and an optional pressure at the cell centres, exactly; a curvilinear one derives Ucat from its fluxes.

The boundary values are this module's own Ubcs. After setting the state it runs the sequence the solved flow is finalized with (see FinalizePostProjectionCellFields): UpdateDummyCells on non-periodic faces, SynchronizePeriodicCellFields on periodic ones, UpdateCornerNodes for edges and corners, and the periodic synchronization again because corner averaging can overwrite periodic endpoints. It does not call that finalizer, which would refresh Ubcs from the boundary handlers.
