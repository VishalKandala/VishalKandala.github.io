7/**
8 * @brief Applies a numerical "test filter" to a 3x3x3 stencil of data points.
9 *
10 * EXPLANATION of "Test Filter":
11 * In the dynamic Smagorinsky LES model the model coefficient is not prescribed. It
12 * is calculated dynamically by sampling the turbulent energy cascade at two
13 * different scales. The first scale is the grid filter itself, implicit in the
14 * discretization. The second, larger scale is this explicit "test filter". By
15 * comparing how the resolved flow behaves at the two scales, the model determines
16 * how much dissipation the unresolved motions require.
17 *
18 * The kernel is selected by the caller rather than read from the simulation
19 * context, so a filter can be exercised on 27 numbers without a live run.
20 *
21 * @param[in] kernel Discrete stencil to apply.
22 * @param[in] values The 3x3x3 array of scalar values at the stencil points,
23 * indexed `[k][j][i]` in the DMDA convention.
24 * @param[in] weights The 3x3x3 array of cell volume weights. Ignored by
25 * ::LES_TEST_FILTER_SIMPSON_IK, which assumes uniform spacing
26 * in its homogeneous plane.
27 * @return The filtered value, or zero when every weight vanishes because the
28 * stencil lies entirely inside a solid body.
29 */
30double ApplyLESTestFilter(LESTestFilterKernel kernel, double values[3][3][3], double weights[3][3][3]);
