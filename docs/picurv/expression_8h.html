Detailed Description

The expression language shared by every configured field, the keyed hashes its random draws and other reproducible draws are built from, and the prescribed velocity sources written in it: their compilation, the face slots a boundary evaluates them at, the face fluxes and cell values they set, and their bulk-velocity scaling.

Callers compile an expression against their own list of variable names and supply the values in that order at evaluation. generators/ic.gen validates the same language; tests/fixtures/expression_conformance.txt holds cases both implementations must agree on.

Compile one expression against a list of variable names.

The grammar is Python's expression syntax restricted to numbers, the given names, pi, arithmetic (+ - * / % **), comparisons (chained ones included), and/or/not, the functions abs atan2 bei ber besselj bessely cos cosh erf exp floor log maximum minimum sin sinh sqrt tan tanh where, the series sum(k, lo, hi, body) over integer bounds, plus uniform and normal when allow_random is true. Comparisons and logical operators yield 1 or 0; % takes the sign of the divisor; ** binds tighter than a unary minus on its left and groups to the right.

Returns
