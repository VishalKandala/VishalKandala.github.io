◆ ProjectInitialVelocity()

static PetscErrorCode ProjectInitialVelocity ( SimCtx *  simCtx)
static

Make the fresh-start velocity divergence-free with the time step's own projection: solve the pressure Poisson equation for the initial Ucont, whose boundary fluxes are already set, and correct the interior faces.

As after a step's projection, the boundary handlers are not reapplied, so the field stays divergence-free. The potential is discarded, so pressure keeps its initial value. Collective.
