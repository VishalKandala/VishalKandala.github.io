)
static

Returns the Clark contribution over the molecular viscous one, per unit x, at one cell.

The field is u_x = x^2 on cubic cells of edge h, so du/dx = 2x and the exact gradient-model stress tau_11 = (h^2/12)(du/dx)^2 varies along x. Both the model's and viscosity's contributions leave Viscous() through the same flux differences, so their ratio does not depend on how Viscous() normalizes its output.
