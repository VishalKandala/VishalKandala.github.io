struct SeedingCellTable

The owned cells a seeding mode places particles in, and which particles each gets.

Volumetric seeding uses the rank's owned cells and surface seeding the owned cells that touch the inlet face, so both are a box of cells from origin with n cells per axis (none when the rank owns no part of the target). Cell c, in i-fastest order, seeds the particles with IDs pid_lo[c] to pid_hi[c] - 1.

Decide which particles each owned cell seeds, the same way at every rank layout.

Collective. The cells are those volumetric (face < 0) or surface seeding uses, and each cell's measure is its volume or its area on the face, integrated with 2x2x2 Gauss points: exact for the volume (the determinant has degree 2 in each coordinate) and for the area of a planar face, where the points coincide in pairs and give the 2x2 rule.

Particles are stratified over the measure in global cell order (i fastest): with P(c) the measure of all cells before cell c, T the total and N the particle count, cell c seeds the IDs floor(N P(c) / T) to floor(N (P(c) + m_c) / T) - 1, so each cell holds its expected share to within one, the IDs are 0..N-1, and both depend only on the grid. For the result to be identical at any rank layout the measures are summed as integers, each cell's measure quantised against the largest cell (an exact maximum) to 2^Q units, with Q chosen so the total fits 63 bits. P(c) is the sum of the (j,k) rows before the cell's row, from one reduction over the rows, plus the cells before it in its row, from a scan over the ranks sharing the row.
