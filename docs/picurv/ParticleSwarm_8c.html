struct SeedingCellTable

The owned cells a seeding mode draws from, with each cell's share of the measure.

Volumetric seeding draws from the rank's owned cells and surface seeding from the owned cells that touch the inlet face, so both are a box of cells from origin with n cells per axis (none when the rank owns no part of the target).

Tabulate the measure of the owned cells a seeding mode draws from.

For face < 0 the cells are the rank's owned cells and the measure is volume; for a face they are the owned cells touching it, and the measure is their area on it, none when the rank does not hold part of the face. cumulative[c] sums cells 0..c in i-fastest order, so a uniform draw on [0, measure) picks a cell in proportion to its measure. The measure integrates the density with 2x2x2 Gauss points, which is exact for the volume (the determinant has degree 2 in each coordinate) and for the area of a planar face; on a face the points coincide in pairs, which gives the 2x2 rule. bound[c] is the largest density on a 3x3x3 lattice of the cell, the envelope used to sample a uniform point inside it.

[in]coorGhosted node-coordinate array.

[out]tableThe owned cells and their measure.
Returns
PetscErrorCode 0 on success; an error for a cell whose measure is not positive.

Draw one seeding point uniform in the measure of a seeding table.

The cell is drawn in proportion to its measure from table->cumulative (see BuildSeedingCellTable); the point inside it is drawn uniform in that measure by accepting uniform logical coordinates with probability density / bound. A surface point is then put just inside the cell, 1e-6 of the cell from the face.
