Tabulate the volumes of this rank's owned cells for volume-uniform seeding.

Cells are visited in i-fastest order from (xs, ys, zs), the cell origins the volumetric seeding uses. cumulative[c] is the summed volume of cells 0..c, so a uniform draw on [0, cumulative[n-1]) picks a cell in proportion to its volume. A cell's volume integrates the trilinear map's Jacobian determinant with 2x2x2 Gauss points, which is exact because the determinant has degree 2 in each coordinate. bound[c] is the largest determinant on a 3x3x3 lattice of the cell, the envelope used to sample a uniform point inside it.

Parameters
[in]userGrid context.
[in]coorGhosted node-coordinate array.
[in]xs,ys,zsOrigin node indices of the rank's first owned cell.
[out]cumulativeAllocated array of cumulative volumes (caller frees), or NULL when not wanted.
[out]boundAllocated array of per-cell determinant bounds (caller frees), or NULL when not wanted.
[out]n_cellsNumber of owned cells.
[out]volumeTotal owned volume.
Returns
PetscErrorCode 0 on success; an error for a cell whose volume is not positive.

Draw one volume-uniform seeding point in this rank's owned cells.

The cell is drawn in proportion to its volume from cell_cdf (see BuildOwnedCellVolumeTable); the point inside it is drawn uniform in physical volume by accepting uniform logical coordinates with probability detJ / cell_bound. On a grid of equal parallelepiped cells this is the uniform-per-cell placement it replaces, which over-filled small cells on stretched or curvilinear grids.
