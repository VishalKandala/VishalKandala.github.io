static

Cartesian gradient of a cell-centred scalar at every owned physical cell.

Each computational derivative is the central difference, or at a non-periodic boundary cell the second-order one-sided difference into the domain, mapped to Cartesian components with the cell's metrics. With lcount, a derivative whose stencil reaches a cell holding no particles is zero, so an empty cell's zero mean does not enter. Writes owned entries only; local to this translation unit.

Parameters
[in]userGrid context.
[in]lscalarGhosted scalar, its ghosts current.
[in]lcountGhosted particle count, its ghosts current; or NULL.
[out]gradientGlobal vector on fda.
