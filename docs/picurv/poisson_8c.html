Detailed Description

Pressure-Poisson projection: operator, right-hand side, multigrid solve, pressure update, and velocity correction.

Grid layout. Cell-centred quantities live at DMDA indices 1..m-2 on each axis; indices 0 and m-1 are dummy layers that carry no unknown. On a periodic axis the interior wraps from m-2 to 1. The contravariant flux Ucont[k][j][i].x sits on the face between cells i and i+1, and likewise for the other two components.

Face gradient. The flux of grad(Phi) through the face between cell c and c + e_n is 

sum_b g_nb D_b(Phi),   g_nb = (F_b . F_n) * aj_n,

where F_b are the contravariant base vectors stored on the n-faces, aj_n is the inverse Jacobian there, D_n is the difference across the face, and D_b (b != n) is the transverse difference chosen by PoissonOperator_TransverseDifference(). The operator assembles the divergence of this flux, and the projection subtracts it from Ucont, so the two are consistent by construction.
