Turn a field value - number, expression, or structured regions - into one expression.

A mapping may declare params (named numbers usable in its expressions and region parameters) and gives either value or background with regions. Regions paint in order, a later region over an earlier one: a sharp region by where(d <= 0, v, before), a smoothed one by before*(1 - s) + v*s, with d the signed distance to the region and s its edge profile over edge.width. The result is validated like any expression.
